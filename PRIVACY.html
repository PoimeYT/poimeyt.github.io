Popcorn — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Popcorn is a watch-together app for the Root platform. You open it inside a Root channel, load a video, and playback, a member list, and chat stay in sync for everyone in the room.

This policy explains what data Popcorn handles and where it goes. It is written to be read, not to cover a lawyer in glory — if anything here is unclear, ask (see Contact).

The short version

  • Popcorn has no accounts, no passwords, no payment, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking.
  • The only personal data it uses is the identity Root already gives it (your user ID and display name, and the channel ID) — to show who's watching, attribute chat messages, and decide who's host.
  • Your room's state — the current video, playback position, room settings, and the last 50 chat messages — is stored as a single record for your channel so people can rejoin where things left off.
  • If you choose to link a Stremio account, your Stremio key stays in your own browser and is never stored on Popcorn's servers.
  • Popcorn does not host or provide video. It plays streams from sources you point it at (a Stremio account, Stremio addons, a YouTube link, or a direct video URL), and those third parties receive requests directly.

What Popcorn is

Popcorn is a synchronization layer. It coordinates playback and chat between people who are already in the same Root channel. It does not host, upload, store, provide, or endorse any video content — what you watch, and where it comes from, is chosen and controlled by the people in the room. A one-time notice on first launch says the same thing.

Data Popcorn handles

Your Root identity

When you open Popcorn in a channel, Root tells the Popcorn server your user ID, your display name, and the channel (room) ID. Popcorn uses this to:

  • show you in the "Watching" list while you're connected,
  • attribute your chat messages, and
  • assign and transfer the host role.

Popcorn does not receive your email address, password, payment details, or any Root profile data beyond the above.

Your room's state

Popcorn keeps one small record per channel so the room survives people leaving and rejoining. It contains:

  • the current video reference (a URL or content ID) and its playback state (playing/paused, position, speed),
  • room settings (e.g. host-only playback),
  • the last 50 chat messages (older messages are discarded — this is a rejoin backfill, not a chat archive), and
  • if a Stremio account is linked, the public addon and catalog URLs derived from it (see below) and the user ID of whoever linked it.

This record is stored in the Root platform's per-app data store, scoped to your channel. It is overwritten as the room changes (new video, new settings, newer chat) and is not shared with other channels.

Live presence

Who is currently connected is held in memory only while people are online and is rebuilt from scratch each session. It is not written to storage.

Stremio account linking (optional)

If the room host links a Stremio account, the host pastes their Stremio authKey. Popcorn's handling of it is deliberately narrow:

  • The authKey is kept in the host's own browser (localStorage) and is sent to the Popcorn server only at the moment of linking.
  • The server uses it to make two calls to api.strem.io — one to validate the account, one to read the list of installed addons — and then discards it. It is never written to disk and never logged.
  • Only the public addon and catalog URLs derived from that list are stored in the room record. Every request after linking (browsing catalogs, resolving streams) goes to those public addon endpoints and carries no authKey.

Data stored in your browser

Popcorn uses your browser's local storage for:

  • a flag recording that you acknowledged the first-launch notice,
  • your saved list of Stremio addon URLs (manual, no-login mode), and
  • the Stremio authKey, if you linked an account (as above).

This data stays in your browser. Clearing your browser data removes it.

What Popcorn does not do

  • No user accounts, logins, or passwords.
  • No payment or financial data.
  • No advertising, no ad networks, no marketing.
  • No analytics, no usage tracking, no cookies for tracking, no fingerprinting.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of data with data brokers.
  • No access to your camera, microphone, files, or location.
  • No profile of you across channels, sessions, or other sites.

Retention

  • Room record: persists in the channel's storage until it is overwritten by newer room activity or removed. Chat within it is always capped at the last 50 messages.
  • Live presence: exists only while you're connected; discarded immediately on disconnect.
  • Stremio authKey: never retained on Popcorn's servers. Retained in your own browser until you clear it.
  • Browser storage: retained until you clear your browser data.

Children

Popcorn is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Popcorn minimizes what it holds: no passwords, no payment data, and no server-side storage of the Stremio authKey. Data in transit between the client and server is handled by the Root platform. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, and Popcorn cannot guarantee absolute security.
