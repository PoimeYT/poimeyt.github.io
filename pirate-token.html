Fan-made parody with fake, free chests. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Epic Games.
Every chest gives a weapon, a heal or shield item, materials and gold bars, like the real ones. Weapon and material odds are rough community numbers.
Epic has never published the real Pirate Shop Access drop rate; estimates range from about 1 in 2,000 to 1 in 700,000. This game uses 1 in 137,000.
Chests take about a second to open in the real game (you hold interact), so that is how long they take here. One chest at a time, no auto-open, no bulk opening. Can't hold a button? Switch "Opening" to tap once: you press once, the chest takes the same second, and you can't start another until it's done.
The 1% chance of a side reward per chest is a community estimate too, and the Solid Gold skins are treated as equally rare as the token.
